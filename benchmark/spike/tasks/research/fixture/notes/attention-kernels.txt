Attention kernel notes (run 14)

Swapped the naive attention for the fused kernel on the 1.3B model.
Throughput went from 1.9k to 4.0k tokens/s, roughly a 2.1x speedup at
sequence length 4096. No change to peak memory worth reporting; the win
here is compute, not memory. Numerics matched the reference to 1e-3.
